About — Ahlan-Trips
Our story

Built for how African businesses actually travel

Ahlan-Trips started where most corporate travel in the region still sits: between a local agency that understands the market but can't report on it, and an international platform that reports beautifully on a market it doesn't know. Neither worked for the organisations we served.

So we built both sides into one company. From Tunis we negotiate directly with airlines and hotel chains — 500+ supplier relationships today — and wrap them in policy, approvals and analytics that a CFO in Tunis, Paris or Dubai can read the same way. Today our programmes cover travel in 140+ countries for more than 5,000 active enterprise clients, with regional operations experts in India, Dubai and Tunisia.

The promise in our logo is the operating principle: your journey, our responsibility. When a flight is cancelled at 02:00, that is our problem to solve, not the traveller's.
